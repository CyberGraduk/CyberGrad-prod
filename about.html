The story

I was you.

I came to the UK as an international student to complete an MSc in Cybersecurity Engineering. I had the technical background, the degree, and the drive. What I didn't have was any understanding of how the UK job market actually works for someone like me.

I did what everyone does. I applied on LinkedIn. Hundreds of applications. Carefully tailored — or so I thought. I heard almost nothing back. Not because I wasn't qualified. But because I didn't know the rules. The unwritten ones nobody puts in a job description.

How UK CVs are formatted differently. What employers actually filter for in ATS systems. Which certifications UK hiring managers care about. How to build a professional network from scratch as an outsider. How to handle visa questions without signalling anxiety.

"I figured it out eventually. But I wasted months learning things nobody should have to learn by trial and error alone."

— Rakesh Elamaran, Founder, CyberGrad

CyberGrad is the resource I wish had existed. Not a bootcamp. Not a course platform. Not a job board. A structured, honest, practical guide — from someone who has been exactly where you are right now.

The programme is deliberately small. Every cohort is 10–20 students maximum. Because this is not about scale for its own sake — it is about giving each student the attention that actually moves the needle on their job search.
