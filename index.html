The Problem

Your academics gave you technical skills.
They didn't give you a roadmap.

Most international cybersecurity graduates apply for months and hear nothing back. Not because they are unqualified. Because nobody told them how UK hiring actually works.

01

CV rejected before anyone reads it

UK ATS systems filter out generic CVs instantly. Most students do not know why they are not getting responses.

02

Applying to roles you cannot get

Government and defence roles require SC clearance. On a Graduate Visa most are off limits. Applying wastes months.

03

Graduate Visa is a countdown clock

2 years. No clear strategy means wasted months before the reality of the deadline actually hits.
