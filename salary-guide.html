UK Cybersecurity Salary Guide

What to expect at each level. Honest ranges based on current UK market data — not inflated figures from recruitment sites.

Important context for international graduates: These are market ranges. As a fresh graduate on a Graduate Visa, expect the lower end initially — especially if you lack UK work experience. Sponsorship often comes with slightly lower starting salaries. After 12–18 months of UK experience, you will be in a much stronger negotiating position.

⚠️ Sponsorship and salary: Roles that offer visa sponsorship often start at the lower end of the range. Some employers use sponsorship as leverage to pay less. Know the market rate — do not accept significantly below range just because they offer sponsorship. You have leverage too.
